YouTube Automation Uploader

YouTube Automation Uploader is a private, internal tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and uploads videos the owner made and approved to the owner's own YouTube channels: Atlas Unboxed, WhyWhy Wobble and GTA 6 Secret Scenes.

It uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload a video and schedule when it goes public,
  • set the video's custom thumbnail,
  • add the video to a playlist on the same channel.

The tool has no other users. It doesn't access anyone else's account, channel or data. It isn't offered to the public.
